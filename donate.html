Support Suchona

Suchona is a volunteer-run, community-supported nonprofit. Every donation helps us host Durga Puja, cultural programs, and gatherings that keep Bengali traditions alive for families across Iowa and Illinois.

  1. Open your bank's mobile app and choose Zelle.
  2. Scan the QR code, or search for Suchona using the email on file.
  3. Enter your donation amount and send — Suchona receives it directly, with no fees.
