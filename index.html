About Us

Suchona, established in 1998, is a registered Non-Profit Organization in Iowa. It was formed to bring together the Indian and Bengali community of Asian Americans settled in Iowa and promote Bengali and South Asian cultural heritage, art, and community welfare. Suchona has played a significant role in supporting the Corridor communities (Cedar Rapids and Iowa City) by sustaining various cultural and social activities.
