About Us

Suchona, established in 1998, is a registered nonprofit organization in Iowa. It was formed to bring together the Indian and Bengali community of Asian Americans settled in Iowa and to promote Bengali and South Asian cultural heritage, art, and community welfare. Suchona has played a significant role in supporting the Corridor communities of Cedar Rapids and Iowa City by sustaining a wide range of cultural and social activities.

Alongside organizing cultural, social, and festive events, the community also supports charitable and volunteer work in the United States, India, and Bangladesh — including Thanksgiving dinners for the homeless, care for elderly cancer patients, and relief for people affected by natural calamities.
