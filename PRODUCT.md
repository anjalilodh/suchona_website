# Suchona Website Product Context

## Product

Suchona is the website for the Bengali Association of Iowa and Illinois, a
registered Iowa nonprofit established in 1998. The site helps community members
understand the organization, discover cultural events, view community media,
and support Suchona through donations.

## Audience

- Bengali and South Asian families in Iowa and Illinois
- Community members looking for cultural events and organization information
- Older visitors who benefit from substantial type, clear contrast, and large
  interaction targets
- Supporters and prospective donors

## Homepage purpose

The homepage should establish Suchona's cultural identity, demonstrate its
community longevity, explain its mission, surface upcoming events, and provide
clear paths to the organization story, all events, and donation.

## Confirmed content constraints

- Preserve the approved hero artwork and composition.
- Use real HTML for all interface copy and controls.
- Keep future community and event photography as explicit placeholders until
  approved images are supplied.
- Treat event dates, locations, membership counts, and annual-event counts as
  placeholders until confirmed.
- Maintain strong readability and keyboard-accessible controls.
